## Prepared behavior

- Default aggressive profile: up to eight children, no recursive delegation,
  shared local command budget from CPU affinity, and up to 24 GiB RAM while
  reserving 4 GiB for the host. This machine currently exposes seven affinity
  CPUs. Remote inference/source lanes can exceed that count; local build jobs
  cannot. Children receive disjoint tasks and independent final review.
- `--profile balanced` restores up to three children/four jobs/12 GiB.
  Explicit agent/job/memory overrides are validated against measured capacity.
- MAKE/CMake/Cargo job defaults reach Codex tool commands through its checked
  shell environment. Memory is a scheduling budget, not a new cgroup control.
  Existing privileged container limits remain 4 CPUs/12 GiB/512 tasks until
  independently reviewed execution profiles authorize a different envelope.
- Startup loads GOAL/START/CONVERGENCE/HANDOFF and records their hashes. The
  exact original objective and existing thread are preserved. M02 and M03
  executable repair are the first priorities after their prerequisites pass.
- Healthy silent commands are preserved by default (`--stall-seconds 0`).
  Worker-heartbeat recovery remains enabled. Recorded stops, unclassified
  pauses/blockers and generic failures do not cause blind retries. Classified
  transport/capacity/rate-limit recovery retains the same thread and backoff.
- Separate child windows open automatically on the local desktop. Each has its
  own readable log and title; display failures are reported with log fallback.
  Closed children retire their viewers; whole-run exit stops following and
  leaves dismissal prompts. Viewing/closing a log does not control its worker.
